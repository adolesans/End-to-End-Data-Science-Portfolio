# End to End Data Science Portfolio

Welcome to the **Data Science** section of my portfolio. Here, I focus on transforming raw data into actionable business intelligence through rigorous Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical inference, and interactive dashboarding.

## Project Index

### 1. EduTrack: Student Lifestyle Analytics
> **Collaborative Project** | *Contribute: Methodology Strategist & Data Scientist *

* **Objective**: To analyze the correlation between student lifestyle choices (sleep, study hours, social activity) and academic performance.
* **Key Contribution**: **Formulated the analytical pipeline** and made critical decisions regarding data preprocessing strategies (handling outliers/missing values) and selecting the appropriate statistical methods to ensure data integrity and insight validity.
* **Tech Stack**: Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Statistical Analysis.
* **🔗 Repository**: [Link to EduTrack](https://github.com/adolesans/End-to-End-Data-Science-Portfolio/tree/main/edutrack_student_lifestyle_analytic)

### 2. Urban Mobility Intelligence
> **Business Intelligence & Dashboarding**

* **Objective**: To optimize bike-sharing operations by forecasting demand based on seasonal and weather data.
* **Key Contribution**: Built an end-to-end analytics pipeline and deployed a **Streamlit** dashboard that visualizes peak usage hours and customer segmentation (Casual vs. Registered), providing strategic insights for fleet management.
* **Tech Stack**: Streamlit, Python, Plotly, Business Intelligence.
* **🔗 Repository**: [Link to Urban Mobility](https://github.com/adolesans/Urban-Mobility-Analytics)
